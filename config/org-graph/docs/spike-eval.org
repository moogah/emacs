#+title: org-graph Spike Evaluation Runbook

This is the hands-on runbook for the ~1-week =org-graph= evaluation
window.  Work through the checklist below during normal day-to-day use,
record what you observe under *Findings*, then answer the *Decision
prompt* at the end.

org-graph is a *spike*: a layered graph/agent surface over the durable
concept vault (=~/org/roam/=) that plugs into the =workspaces= substrate
and runs alongside org-roam without touching it.  The point of this
window is to learn what is actually load-bearing before committing to a
long-term shape (see =openspec/changes/org-graph-spike/proposal.md=).
Every resolved design decision (=RE-1=..=RE-6=, =RE-2a=) should get
exercised and stress-tested here; every open question (=OQ1=..=OQ5= in
=design.md= § Open Questions) should get an empirical answer.

* How to use this runbook

- Each =[ ]= item names the exact command, function, menu key, or
  property to invoke and the observable result that means "pass".
  Check it off when you have seen the expected result with your own
  eyes; if it fails or surprises you, leave it unchecked and write the
  surprise under *Findings*.
- Where an item says "from a fresh boot", it means launch a brand-new
  Emacs (e.g. the GUI =Emacs.app= for this worktree, or
  =./bin/emacs-isolated.sh=), NOT a =M-x jf/reload-module=.  Several
  checks specifically test work that only runs at startup — see
  *Boot model and the reload caveat* immediately below.
- "Eval" means =M-:= (=eval-expression=) for a one-off form, or place
  point after a form in =*scratch*= and =C-x C-e=.

* Boot model and the reload caveat (read before the Discovery checks)

org-graph keeps cold module load *DB-free*: =(require 'org-graph)= loads
all eight submodules in canonical order but touches no database
(invariant: =register/invariant/org-graph-loader-ordered-sequence=).
Two DB-touching operations are therefore *deferred* to
=emacs-startup-hook= — which is the post-init seam under =emacs -q
--load init.el= (NOT =after-init-hook=):

1. Typed-edge extractor *registration* — =org-graph--register-extractor=
   wraps =org-graph-extractor-register=, which applies the =typed_edges=
   schema and opens the vulpea DB.
2. The =org-id-locations= *seed* — =org-graph--seed-org-id-locations-deferred=
   wraps =org-graph/seed-org-id-locations=, which queries the vulpea DB.

These two are only *wiring-tested* by the automated suite (the suite
asserts they are on the hook and drives them with the DB stubbed); their
actual firing against a real DB at real launch is NOT covered by tests.
That is exactly why this runbook verifies them by hand below.

- [ ] *Caveat acknowledged:* re-running =M-x jf/reload-module RET
  org-graph RET= mid-session does NOT re-run the two deferred ops (they
  live on =emacs-startup-hook=, which has already fired).  A *restart*
  is required to re-test them — or call the deferred functions directly
  (=M-x org-graph--register-extractor=, =M-x
  org-graph--seed-org-id-locations-deferred=).  Do not mistake a
  mid-session reload that "loses" registration for a registration bug.

* Live-session firing of deferred work (RE-2, loader invariant)

These prove =emacs-startup-hook= fired and the deferred ops landed
against a *real* DB on a normal boot.

- [ ] *Module loaded and registered (cold load).* From a fresh boot,
  eval =(featurep 'org-graph)= → non-nil, and =(fboundp
  'org-graph/find-topic)= → =t=.  org-graph loads on a normal boot with
  no manual step (backed by =config/org-graph/test/module-load-spec.el=).
- [ ] *Typed-edge extractor actually registered.* From a fresh boot,
  eval =(vulpea-db-get-extractor 'org-graph-typed-edges)= → non-nil.
  Non-nil means =emacs-startup-hook= fired and the extractor +
  =typed_edges= schema landed against the real vulpea DB.
- [ ] *org-id seed ran — cross-workspace =id:= links resolve.* From a
  fresh boot (no manual re-index, no opening the target files first),
  follow an =id:= link that points from one workspace / =~/org/roam/=
  into a note that lives in a *different* indexed root (=C-c C-o= on the
  link, or eval =(org-id-find "<that-id>")=).  It resolves to the file.
  This proves =org-graph/seed-org-id-locations= populated the global
  =org-id-locations= from the vulpea DB at startup.
- [ ] *Warnings are clean.* Check the =*Warnings*= buffer after boot for
  =org-id-locations seed skipped= or =typed-edge extractor registration
  skipped= warnings (both deferred ops report failures via
  =display-warning=, which writes to =*Warnings*=, NOT =*Messages*=).
  Their absence means neither deferred op hit its resilient error guard.

* Capability: Discovery (vulpea-only) — RE-1, RE-2, RE-2a

vulpea is the single index, fed *explicit, bounded* roots from the
workspace registry plus =~/org/roam/= — never a wholesale =~/work= walk.

- [ ] *Roots are explicit and bounded.* Eval =(org-graph/index-roots)=.
  The returned list contains =~/org/roam/= (=org-graph-roam-root=) and
  the =:home= of each *active* workspace (=org-graph--active-workspace-homes=),
  and nothing else.
- [ ] *=~/work= is NOT walked wholesale.* Confirm =(org-graph/index-roots)=
  and =vulpea-db-sync-directories= do NOT contain =~/work/= or any bare
  project-repo root you did not create as a workspace.  (RE-1/RE-2: we
  watch only the handful of workspace homes you actually created.)
- [ ] *Workspace home auto-gets an =:ID:= (RE-2a).* Create a new
  workspace (via the normal workspaces creation flow).  Open its
  scaffolded =home.org= and confirm it has an =:ID:= property in the
  top PROPERTIES drawer (it was =org-id-get-create='d at birth, so it
  is indexable by vulpea).  Same for a freshly created =sessions/*.org=
  session file.
- [ ] *Birth-indexing — new home is indexed immediately (=:on-create=).*
  Right after creating that workspace, query for it WITHOUT any manual
  re-index: eval =(vulpea-db-query (lambda (n) (string-match-p "<home-basename>"
  (vulpea-note-path n))))= (or run =M-x org-graph/find-any= and look for
  the new =home.org=).  It appears.  This is the =:on-create= handler
  calling =vulpea-db-sync-update-directory= — a *one-shot index at
  birth*.
- [ ] *Ongoing pickup — the load-bearing ergonomics question.* This is
  the distinction the spike exists to settle.  Into that same new
  workspace home, add a *new* note file (with an =:ID:=) AFTER the
  workspace already existed.  WITHOUT any manual step, query for it
  (=org-graph/find-any= / =vulpea-db-query=).
  - Sub-check (a) — *does it appear on its own?*  =vulpea-db-sync-update-directory=
    is a one-shot index; it does NOT install a live filenotify watcher
    for a directory added after autosync started, so the expectation is
    that a *later*-added note does NOT auto-appear.
  - Sub-check (b) — *does the menu re-index pick it up?*  Now run the
    Integrations menu "G" entry (next section) or =M-x
    org-graph/configure-sync=, then re-query.  It now appears.
  - Record under *Findings*: is "indexed-at-birth + manual re-index on
    demand" acceptable ergonomically, or does =:on-create= need to
    trigger a full autosync *restart* so later notes are watched live?
- [ ] *Integrations menu "G".*  From INSIDE a workspace (the Integrations
  group =user-error=s outside one, and the =org-graph= entry will be
  absent — that is expected, not a failure), open the workspaces
  transient with =M-x workspace-menu=, enter its Integrations group, and
  confirm an =org-graph= entry is bound to key ="G"=.  Invoking it runs
  =org-graph-workspace-integration--menu= → =org-graph/configure-sync=,
  which re-reads the live registry and re-indexes the current roots
  (returns =ok=; no error).

* Capability: Taxonomy (vulpea-schema) — RE-3

Note types =(log debug topic reference project)= are =vulpea-schema=
definitions (registered by =org-graph-schemas-register=), with a filetag
predicate and field expectations — not bare filetags.

- [ ] *Tag one note of each type.* In =~/org/roam/=, create or tag five
  notes, one per type, using the type filetag (=#+filetags: :log:= ,
  =:debug:=, =:topic:=, =:reference:=, =:project:=).  Each note has an
  =:ID:=.  Let vulpea sync (or run =org-graph/configure-sync=).
- [ ] *Per-type finders return the right notes.*  Run each of =M-x
  org-graph/find-topic=, =org-graph/find-debug=, =org-graph/find-log=,
  =org-graph/find-reference=, =org-graph/find-project=.  Each lists the
  note(s) you tagged with that type and excludes the others.  =M-x
  org-graph/find-any= lists all of them.
- [ ] *Validation surfaces violations.*  For each type, eval
  =(org-graph/validate-all-of-type 'topic)= (and =log= / =debug= /
  =reference= / =project=).  Eyeball the returned violations against the
  notes you tagged.  Per-note spot check: =(org-graph/validate-note-type
  NOTE)=.
- [ ] *Judge the schema fit (feeds the follow-up).*  For each type ask:
  is the schema's field expectation set *right*, too *strict* (flags
  notes you consider valid), or too *loose* (passes notes you consider
  malformed)?  Record per-type under *Findings* — this is the primary
  RE-3 evaluation.

* Capability: Typed edges — RE-4, D2, D3

Authored as PROPERTIES-drawer entries; extraction is *roam-only*
(=~/org/roam/=).  Relation symbols: =(implements contradicts supersedes
relates-to)= → keys =:IMPLEMENTS:= / =:CONTRADICTS:= / =:SUPERSEDES:= /
=:RELATES_TO:=.

- [ ] *Author edges on 3-5 concept notes.*  On 3-5 existing =~/org/roam/=
  concept notes, add typed-edge properties in the PROPERTIES drawer,
  e.g.:
  #+begin_example
  :PROPERTIES:
  :ID:           <uuid>
  :IMPLEMENTS:   [[id:abc]]
  :RELATES_TO:   [[id:def]] [[id:ghi]]
  :END:
  #+end_example
  Use at least one each of =:IMPLEMENTS:=, =:CONTRADICTS:=,
  =:SUPERSEDES:=, =:RELATES_TO:= across the set.  Let vulpea re-index.
- [ ] *Outgoing query.*  Eval =(org-graph-query/outgoing "<from-id>")=
  for a note you edited → returns the edges you authored *on* that note.
  Optionally filter: =(org-graph-query/outgoing "<from-id>" 'implements)=.
- [ ] *Incoming query.*  Eval =(org-graph-query/incoming "<to-id>")= for
  a note pointed *at* → returns the edges authored elsewhere that target
  it.  Confirm it is directional (NOT auto-symmetrized with outgoing).
- [ ] *Connected query.*  Eval =(org-graph-query/connected "<note-id>")=
  → returns the union of outgoing + incoming for that note.
- [ ] *Project notes do NOT get typed edges (D2 / roam-only).*  Add an
  =:IMPLEMENTS:= property to a *project* note that lives OUTSIDE
  =~/org/roam/= (e.g. in a workspace home).  Re-index, then query its id
  with =org-graph-query/outgoing= → returns nothing.  Extraction is
  gated to roam notes (=org-graph-extractor--roam-note-p=).

* Capability: Agent surface — RE-5, register/boundary/org-graph-agent-tools

The per-workspace =workspace-assistant= exposes exactly three gptel
tools (snake_case =:name=s): =org_graph_query=, =org_graph_typed_edges=,
=org_graph_write_node=.  The reusable accessor is =org-graph/agent-tools=.

- [ ] *Fresh-workspace assistant LISTS the tools.*  From a fresh boot,
  create a new workspace and open its =workspace-assistant= chat.  Ask
  the assistant to list its available tools (or inspect its preset).
  All three of =org_graph_query=, =org_graph_typed_edges=,
  =org_graph_write_node= are present.  This confirms the =:on-create= +
  preset-population path (=org-graph-workspace-integration--populate-assistant-tools=)
  fired in a live boot.  (Sanity-eval: =(org-graph/agent-tools)= →
  three =gptel-tool= objects.)
- [ ] *=org_graph_query= read.*  From the workspace assistant, ask it to
  query the graph (e.g. "list notes tagged topic" / search by title).
  It returns matching notes (routes through =org-graph-tools/query=).
- [ ] *=org_graph_typed_edges= read.*  Ask the assistant for the typed
  edges of one of the concept notes you edited above.  It returns the
  outgoing/incoming/connected edges (routes through
  =org-graph-tools/typed-edges=).
- [ ] *=org_graph_write_node= stamps =:agent-draft:=.*  Ask the
  assistant to create a new note via its write tool.  Open the created
  file: it carries the =agent-draft= filetag (=org-graph-tools/write-node=
  always adds it).  Confirm it surfaces in =M-x
  org-graph/find-agent-drafts=.
- [ ] *No file corruption under rapid calls (coordinator lock).*  Drive
  several rapid agent writes (or eval several =org-graph-tools/write-node=
  calls in quick succession, and/or repeated writes to the same target).
  All resulting files are well-formed org (no interleaved/truncated
  content).  Writes route through =org-graph-coordinator/with-file-lock=,
  the confirmed *unconditional* per-path lock (busy-waits up to
  =org-graph-coordinator-timeout=, default 5.0s, then signals
  =org-graph-coordinator-lock-timeout=).

* Capability: Coexistence with org-roam — D8

org-roam runs unchanged throughout; independent DBs, shared on-disk
format.

- [ ] *org-roam UX unchanged.*  Use =org-roam-node-find=,
  =org-roam-node-insert=, the backlinks buffer, and dailies as you
  normally would.  Behaviour is identical to before the spike; no new
  prompts, errors, or slowdowns attributable to org-graph.
- [ ] *Independent state.*  Confirm the vulpea DB lives at
  =runtime/state/vulpea/notes.db= and org-roam keeps its own DB path —
  rebuilding one never disturbs the other.

* Open questions to answer empirically (design.md § Open Questions)

Answer these from real usage during the window, not pre-emptively.

- [ ] *OQ1 — Cheap startup variant?*  Does the boot-time =org-id-locations=
  seed (=org-graph/seed-org-id-locations=) feel instant, or does startup
  visibly stall?  Confirm it does NOT trigger a recursive directory walk
  (it reads from =(vulpea-db-query)=, not the filesystem).  Note whether
  a cheaper/lazier variant is warranted.
- [ ] *OQ2 — Right default for watched roots per machine?*  Note whether
  the active workspace homes + =~/org/roam/= scope feels right on this
  machine (apploi-mac vs personal-mac have different =~/work= shapes).
  Record whether a machine-role override in =config/local/<role>.el= is
  needed.
- [ ] *OQ3 — =:agent-draft:= finder exclusion: defcustom or hard-coded?*
  After a week of drafts accumulating, decide whether the type finders
  (=topic= / =reference= especially) should hide =agent-draft= notes by
  default, and whether that belongs behind a defcustom.  (Note: as built
  there is =org-graph/find-agent-drafts= but no exclusion toggle on the
  other finders yet — record the felt need.)
- [ ] *OQ4 — Is =RELATES_TO= too broad?*  Watch how often you reach for
  =:RELATES_TO:= as a catch-all vs the precise three (=IMPLEMENTS= /
  =CONTRADICTS= / =SUPERSEDES=).  Decide after ~two weeks: drop it to
  force precision, keep it, or add domain-specific relation types
  (extend =org-graph-relation-types=).
- [ ] *OQ5 — vulpea-journal: add value or skip?*  If =vulpea-journal= is
  installed (unbound), evaluate whether it complements or fragments your
  existing org-roam dailies.  Decide: adopt, drop, or leave provisional.
- [ ] *RE-6 — vulpea 2.4 maturity holds up?*  RE-6 rests on vulpea 2.4
  features: the parser epoch, automatic DB rebuild on schema bumps,
  async-by-default sync, and =vulpea-doctor=.  Over the window, confirm
  each behaves as relied upon: (a) =M-x vulpea-doctor= reports a healthy
  DB; (b) async sync does not visibly stall the UI on save; (c) a schema
  bump triggers an automatic rebuild (no manual DB delete).  Record any
  feature that did NOT behave as the design assumed.
- [ ] *RE-6 — Parser-epoch actually repopulates =typed_edges=?*  After
  changing the extractor (or on a schema/extractor version bump), confirm
  the parser epoch invalidates the file cache and =typed_edges= is rebuilt
  — i.e. re-querying =org-graph-query/outgoing= reflects edited edges
  without a manual full resync.
- [ ] *Do un-ID'd notes still slip through?*  vulpea only indexes notes
  with an =:ID:=.  Spot-check whether notes you *expected* indexed are
  silently missing because they lack an =:ID:= (workspace homes /
  sessions should be auto-ID'd per RE-2a; hand-authored notes may not
  be).  Record any surprising omissions.

* Decision prompt

At the end of the window, pick the long-term shape and record the
evidence that points to it.  This is the input to the follow-up change
(=proposal.md=: findings inform the decision on long-term shape).

- *Keep both (org-roam + org-graph layered).*  Pointed to if: the
  taxonomy/typed-edges/agent surface earn their keep, org-roam UX stayed
  clean (Coexistence checks all passed), and the dual-DB cost was
  invisible in practice.
- *Retire org-roam (org-graph/vulpea becomes primary).*  Pointed to if:
  vulpea-backed discovery + schema taxonomy fully subsumed your org-roam
  workflows, =id:= resolution was reliable across a fresh boot, and
  running two indices felt purely redundant.
- *Fold org-graph into workspaces.*  Pointed to if: the load-bearing
  value was the per-workspace agent surface and birth-indexing, the
  typed-edge/concept-vault layer saw little use, and the natural home
  for what remained is the workspace integration itself.
- *Abandon the spike.*  Pointed to if: the taxonomy felt mis-fit
  (RE-3), typed edges went unused or =RELATES_TO= collapsed into noise
  (OQ4), or the ergonomics of manual re-index / deferred-op fragility
  outweighed the benefit.  Rollback is cheap: remove =org-graph= from
  =jf/enabled-modules=, delete =config/org-graph/= and
  =runtime/state/vulpea/=; authored PROPERTIES are inert without the
  extractor.

Capture, for whichever option you choose, the *specific checklist items
and Findings* that drove the call.
